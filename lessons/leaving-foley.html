Leaving Foley

January 23 – February 5, 2024 · Foley, Alabama → Tallahassee, Florida · Posted from the road on Day 1,293 · 66,695.3 documented miles — this tour

LEAVING FOLEY — the film, 4:58 · also on the Ride Or Die America Page

January 23, 2024. After two months off the bike, he rolled out of Foley, Alabama weak — his word — begging for his strength back. The restart didn’t ease him in. It sent weather.

Night one, Gulf State Park: a fellow camper — no name, just belief — put $40 in his hand. Then the wind at the Flora-Bama line, and he rode straight into the storm anyway. Rain found him on a church porch. A door opened at Jeb’s in the Gonzalez area. Blackwater River ran cold under the pines.

In Crestview, Little Cub’s — a hostel for Florida Scenic Trail hikers — took in the first biker they had ever hosted, and made him breakfast in the morning. Somebody always shows up.

Then the cold dropped: 39 degrees, then 37. Pastor Forrest prayed over him at Hillcrest Baptist. Jan waved from a driveway in Panama City. In David Blair’s woods outside Blountstown he likely took a zero — his words: “I think I took a zero day there, but I’m not sure.”

When the rig needed hands in Tallahassee, Trey drove two hours each way — four hours round trip, in the dark — with goodies. On February 5 he rolled into the Bicycle House, Tallahassee, strength coming back mile by mile. On the way to Key West.

The film carries its own original song, “RIDE OR DIE IS BACK” — his own words, caught on his own tape twice in this stretch: at the Flora-Bama, and at a campfire six days later. The chorus carries the whole doctrine of the comeback: “Weak is where you start — it ain’t where you end.”

The lesson — It is not all sunshine and rainbows. A comeback doesn’t start strong. It starts honest: two months off, riding weak, into a storm — and the road keeps sending angels to a man who keeps pedaling.

R.O.D.A. — Ride Out Depleted Anyway.
