Then The Wind Came Around.

November 16, 2026 · Jesup, Georgia · Day 1,294 · 66,733.4 documented miles — this tour.

Then The Wind Came Around — the film, 3:02

Day 1,294 opened the way the hook says it: freezing to death at 17 miles an hour. A quiet day — he barely talked out there, and the film doesn’t pretend otherwise. Thirty-eight-point-one miles, Midway to Jesup, and for the first 20 of them the crosswind did the talking.

The day’s receipts are two pearls and a turn. “At the stop sign, turn right on to Georgia 119, alright?” And then the sand: “Sandy dirt roads — let’s go.” Somewhere past mile 20, the wind that had kicked him all morning came around — and pushed.

The film carries its own original song, “THEN THE WIND CAME AROUND” — a duet written for this day and no other, built on the one line worth repeating under load: then the wind came around.

Then The Wind Came Around — the sizzle cut, 1:00

Day 5 of the 50-day Proving Season — 46 days to January 1. He rolled into Jesup froze, quiet, and still moving. That’s the whole doctrine in one day: the standard doesn’t wait for better weather.

The lesson — THE DISCIPLINE: Don’t quit in the crosswind. Hold on — it turns. The wind that kicks you for 20 miles is the same wind that pushes you home — if you’re still on the road when it comes around.
