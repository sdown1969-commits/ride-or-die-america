Still Dedicated

June 22 – July 3, 2024 · Sheridan Lake, Colorado → Canon City, Colorado · Posted from the road on Day 1,297 · 66,769.6 documented miles — this tour

STILL DEDICATED — Film #6 Chapter One: The Silent Crossing, the film, 3:47 · also on the Ride Or Die America Page · YouTube · Instagram · TikTok · Coach’s profile

June 22, 2024. He crossed the Colorado line at Sheridan Lake and told nobody — the sign selfie sat in his phone for four days. His words for it: “Silence is golden sometimes. I just had to get through it and get to it.”

On June 24 he took a zero day on holy ground: the little white church in Sheridan Lake, a hiker-biker hostel on the TransAm — he always tried to take a zero day on holy ground. Two days later in Ordway his feet were in a backyard pond at the coffee shop — ducks, geese, chickens, good coffee — while it ran 105 degrees in the shade.

In Fowler he was taken in by Sheryll, Floyd, and Heidi Ridgway — Bike Angels who have hosted him three or four times over the years, and not just him: everybody on that trail. On June 30 he had church service in a park in Florence, on a Sunday, nobody there knowing his name. Blessed and favored for sure.

Then three slow days on the Arkansas at Canon City — a primitive campsite by the river, and a night on church grounds or at a convent; he can’t remember which, and the record keeps it that way. On July 3 he climbed out of Canon City to a primitive campsite in the mountains and lit his own fire. His words that night: “It’s July 3rd, and tomorrow once again I live a life of freedom and independence. There’s nobody — it’s me. Ride or die.”

The chapter carries its own original duet, written for this film: “STILL DEDICATED.” The man’s voice is where you’ve been — the silence, the heat, the doubt. The woman’s voice is where you’re going — the dawn, the worthiness, the belief. The chorus is both of them together, and it is the line this chapter leaves in your hands:

“Isolated, still dedicated.”

The lesson — THE DISCIPLINE: Nobody’s coming — get up anyway. Do today’s one thing completely alone, on purpose, and don’t post it, don’t announce it, don’t tell anybody. Just be dedicated in the quiet. Dedication that needs company isn’t dedication — it’s negotiation.

R.O.D.A. — Ride Out Depleted Anyway.
