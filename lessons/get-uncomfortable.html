Get Uncomfortable

March 11 – April 25, 2024 · Lloyd, Florida → the Alabama–Tennessee line · Posted from the road on Day 1,294 · 66,733.4 documented miles — this tour

GET UNCOMFORTABLE — the film, 6:08 · also on the Ride Or Die America Page

March 11, 2024. He rolled straight out of Kathy’s in Lloyd, Florida, back toward Tallahassee and down to the St. Marks Trailhead — six weeks of road ahead, a puppy growing by the week, and no idea the stretch would end with a JB weld holding his whole life together.

Mar 13, at a park: Jethro said “I’ll be right back,” left in his fancy Jeep — and came back with $220. An hour later he named it on tape: “Today the universe is provided bike angel, bike magic.” The doubt rode too — and that same evening, the dog earned a rib.

Then nine days of silence — repairs on the bike and the trailer at Ken James’s house in Foley, while a storm cancelled the ferries for two days. Ken rode with him to the ferry — bikes together — across Mobile Bay to Dauphin Island. One night under a stranger’s house. Out the next morning into a 20-mile-an-hour headwind.

Mar 29, Mississippi: Freda Tolar — again. The host from Shepard State Park in 2020, standing by his rig three and a half years later. One trail the whole way: the Longleaf, through Hattiesburg. Apr 5: a judge in Mississippi — “best place, best host on the planet.” A private concert, food, and a roof.

Apr 20: a bikers-only campground on the Natchez Trace, two days holding for a storm — and the five-years monologue: “I am happier living like this on the road in my little compound than I ever was living in million-dollar houses.” Apr 24, the steak camp: “It’s my little room, there’s my dining room, there’s my kitchen — there’s my house.”

Apr 25: the state line, in frame. That night, just outside Duck River, Tennessee — as he recalls it — the trailer broke, the bike seat broke, and the JB weld held. Beans and bagels for dinner. The new trailer waits somewhere in Kentucky. That’s the next stretch.

The film carries its own original song, “GET UNCOMFORTABLE” — built on his own words from this leg: the chorus he said over a breakdown dinner, and the monologue he recorded under the storm hold.

“Suffering is growth — get uncomfortable.”

The lesson — Suffering is growth. The road doesn’t hand you easy; it hands you exactly the break, the headwind, and the weight that build the strength you prayed for — if you refuse to set it down.
