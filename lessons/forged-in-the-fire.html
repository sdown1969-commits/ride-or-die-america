Forged in the Fire

July 4 – July 13, 2024 · Hartsel, Colorado → Rand, Colorado · Posted from the road on Day 1,297 · 66,769.6 documented miles — this tour

FORGED IN THE FIRE — Film #6 Chapter Two: Back in the Mountains, the film, 4:19 · also on the Ride Or Die America Page · YouTube · YouTube Short · Instagram · TikTok · Coach’s profile

July 4, 2024. He spent the Fourth in Hartsel, Colorado — a little biker campsite behind the bar, great food, good people. Two nights later he was camped in the pines above Fairplay at 10,000 feet, and it was colder than shit — so he ran a fire all night to keep from freezing. His words for that night: “That’s how you become forged in the fire.”

You know this stretch of your own life. It’s the season where you finally climb back on top of something — the job, the sober stretch, the body, the business — and the moment you stand there proud of yourself, life answers. The flat tire on the way down. The storm out of nowhere. The cold morning nobody warned you about. The summit isn’t the test. What the summit does to you is.

On July 8 he climbed CO-9 to Hoosier Pass — no shoulder the whole way, trucks and RVs and campers flying past at 60, 70, 80, 90 with inches to spare, the wind trying to knock him off the mountain. He stood at the top proud and confident — and as he started down, on what was supposed to be a 70-mile day, he got a flat tire and a pump that water and heat and time had killed. Camped in Breckenridge instead.

On July 9 a storm rolled in out of nowhere while he camped down by the river. His answer: “It’s okay — I flow like water.” On July 11 he soaked at Hot Sulphur Springs — a planned stop, camped a quarter mile from the pools — and came out healed and rejuvenated. And on July 13 he woke up freezing in the dark below Willow Creek Pass, hauled 300 pounds of gear and a trailer over his second Continental Divide in five days, and rolled through Rand one day out from Wyoming: “Coming for you, Wyoming.”

The chapter carries its own original duet, written for this film: “FORGED IN THE FIRE.” The man’s voice is where you’ve been — the pride, the storm, the cold. The woman’s voice is where you’re going — the dawn, the worthiness, the belief. The chorus is both of them together, and it is the line this chapter leaves in your hands:

“Forged in the fire.”

The lesson — THE DISCIPLINE: Get up before the sun tomorrow. The coldest, darkest hour is the assignment — be up in it on purpose, watch the sun come up, and start the day already won. You can’t ever get super confident — the top is where you check the ego, not feed it. And the harder it is, the better it is: the more uncomfortable, the more gratitude. Some days you ride, some days you thrive, some days you survive. The fire doesn’t finish you — it forges you.

R.O.D.A. — Ride Out Depleted Anyway.
