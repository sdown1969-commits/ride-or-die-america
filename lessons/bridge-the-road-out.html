October 28, 2026 · Leaving Myrtle Beach, South Carolina · Filmed on Day 1,275 · 66,511.4 documented miles

THE ROAD OUT — the film, 2:06

October 28, 2026. Two minutes and six seconds of pure riding — Coach RODA rolling out of Myrtle Beach, shot on his GoPro, scored with an original song written for the film: "The Road Out."

The song says it the way he lives it: "This ain't running, this is riding. There's a difference and the blacktop knows. Every mile is a page I'm writing. Every town's a ghost I outgrow."

Running is away-from. Riding is toward. Running panics; riding decides. Same road, same man — the direction of the heart is the whole difference.

And near the end, the line the whole journey hangs on: "I ain't lost. I'm just between the man I was and mine."

The question this one leaves with you: what are YOU riding away from — and what's the difference between running and riding?
