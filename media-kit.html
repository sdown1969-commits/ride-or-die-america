The Story

Coach RODA (Sean Charles Downing) is a transformation coach riding solo and unsupported across America — a journey that began May 3, 2023 and is still rolling. To date he has ridden 66,511.4 documented miles (verified through Oct 30, 2026) over 1,250+ journey days: desert heat, mountain passes, hurricanes, and, in May 2025, a collision with a semi truck — after which he kept riding.

Coach is not an influencer and does not sell audience size. His product is human transformation; endurance is the evidence; the bicycle is the delivery vehicle. The road is his laboratory — daily proof, documented in video, photography, and ride logs from some of the most remote routes in America.
