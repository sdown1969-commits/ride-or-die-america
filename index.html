The road is the laboratory. Transformation is the mission.

Coach RODA — Sean Charles Downing — left on May 3, 2023, solo and unsupported, and he is still out there. No chase car. No finish-line photo op. Just a loaded e-bike, a trailer, and a decision he remakes every single morning: keep going, and outgrow yesterday.

Every mile is proof before philosophy. The lessons on this page were not written in an office — they were earned in headwinds, on ferry decks, in small towns, and on the mornings when quitting would have been easier. In May 2025 he was struck by a semi — and continued afterward. That is the standard this work is held to.

Proof before philosophy. Ride On. Defy Average. Become Undeniable.

Right now the road runs south: down the East Coast toward Key West — a checkpoint, not the end. Then inland to the Arkansas High Country, down to South Padre Island, up through the Utah Cliffs and Idaho to the border, and north to Prudhoe Bay, Alaska. Ride or die forever.

Night One — Where It All Started

"You wouldn't believe me if I told you." He left Las Vegas that morning, 50 years old, on a loaded bicycle. By dark he had fought 30-mile headwinds gusting to 50, made about 30 miles all day, and camped in an abandoned mining camp — a ghost town with the lights left on. He did not quit that night. 66,439.9 documented miles later (through Oct 24), he still hasn't. The full story is in the book — OUTGROW YESTERDAY, coming soon.

Questions people ask about Coach RODA

The short answers — who he is, what Ride Or Die America and The Road Code are, and how to book, follow, or support the ride.

Who is Coach RODA?

Coach RODA is Sean Charles Downing — a motivational trainer by bicycle and transformation mindset trainer. Since May 3, 2023 he has ridden solo and unsupported across America with Ride Or Die America: 66,000+ miles and 1,290 days on the road, sharing one lesson from the road every day.

What is Ride Or Die America?

Ride Or Die America is Coach RODA's solo ride across America and the brand built on it. Its rule: transformation is the product, endurance is the evidence, and cycling is the delivery vehicle. Its motto is OUTGROW YESTERDAY.

What is The Road Code?

The Road Code is Coach RODA's framework for personal accountability, daily discipline, and identity transformation through physical exertion. It runs as a daily lesson and a weekly rhythm — One Word Monday, a lesson Tuesday, Win Wednesday, Proof Thursday, Member Spotlight Friday, a Saturday Challenge, and a Sunday Reset — in the Ride Or Die America — The Road Code Facebook group and across his channels.

How many miles has Coach RODA ridden?

66,000+ miles over 1,290 days on the road, riding solo and unsupported since May 3, 2023. His e-bike, which he calls The Beast, has 23,000+ miles on it under him, and he hauls his gear in a trailer he calls The Sherpa.

Where is Coach RODA riding now?

He is in coastal Georgia — Richmond Hill / Savannah — riding south down the East Coast toward Key West, Florida — a checkpoint, not the end of the journey. From there: inland to the Arkansas High Country, down to South Padre Island, Texas, up through the Utah Cliffs and Idaho to the border, and north to Prudhoe Bay, Alaska.

How can I book Coach RODA to speak or partner with Ride Or Die America?

For motivational speaking, media interviews, and paid brand partnerships, send a message through the Ride Or Die America Facebook Page. Coach RODA delivers keynotes and transformation mindset training built on The Road Code.

How can I follow or support the ride?

Follow Ride Or Die America on Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, and TikTok (@rideordieamerica), and join The Road Code group for the daily lesson. Coach rides in support of the Wounded Warrior Project, St. Jude's, and the American Breast Cancer Society — his GoFundMe, Powering My Charity Ride Across America, helps fund the off-grid solar system, backup battery, and gear that keep the ride moving.
